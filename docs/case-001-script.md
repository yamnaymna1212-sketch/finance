# Case File 001 — Rent vs Own · Voiceover script

Read it calm and sure. Short pause at every period. About 48 seconds.
Numbers come from `previews/case-001-rent-vs-own/model.py`.

Same house. Same street.
The owner is broke. The renter isn't.

Meet the renter. He pays twenty-two hundred a month. That's it.
Pipe breaks? The landlord pays.
Every month, he invests about a thousand dollars. S&P 500. No skipping.

Now the owner.
Fifty-two thousand down, just to get the keys.
Then the bills. Mortgage. Taxes. Insurance. Repairs.
Over a thousand more than rent. Every month.

Year four. The roof goes.
No cash left. It goes on a card.

Ten years later? Almost a tie.
But only one of them can touch his money.
Stay twenty years, and the owner wins. Rent keeps going up.

So, rent or own?

## Video description (put the assumptions here)
$400K home, 10% down + 3% closing, 6.5% 30-year loan, rent $2,200, 7% stock return, 3.5% yearly growth. Stocks after 15% tax on gains, house after 6% selling costs. The $12,000 roof is an example. Not financial advice.
