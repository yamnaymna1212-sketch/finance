# The Gold Mind — working rules

Faceless US-audience finance channel. Black + gold. Line-art pawn characters: Gold vs Ash.
The user writes in Arabic; reply in Arabic. Scripts, hooks and on-screen text are simple American English.

## Video workflow (always)

1. Deliver a 6-second preview first (vertical 1080x1920 MP4). Stop there.
2. Only build the full video after the user says to continue ("كمل").
3. The first frame IS the hook: a short, shocking contradiction in plain American words. No intro, no logo first.

## Scene style ("Same Street")

- Two identical American suburban houses side by side on the same street (gable roof, porch, windows, fence, mailbox).
- Two characters, one per side. A label floats above each roof, e.g. RENT (ash grey) and OWNER (gold).
- One variable changes. Show both sides' costs. Assumptions on screen. No cherry-picked periods.
- Debt = red weight. Savings = gold jar/pile. Year counter shows time passing.
- Do not copy other channels' characters, art or title lines.
